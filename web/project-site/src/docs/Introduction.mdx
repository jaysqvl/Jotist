# Introduction

Jotist is a self-hosted audio transcription workspace built on [Scriberr](https://github.com/rishikanthc/Scriberr), with local models, speaker detection, notes, and optional cloud integrations.

Jotist can transcribe audio and video locally on your machine. If you choose a cloud transcription or AI provider, the relevant audio or transcript is sent to that provider.
Using speech recognition models (such as **Whisper**, **NVIDIA Parakeet**, and **Canary**), it delivers high-accuracy text with word-level timing.

Jotist goes beyond simple transcription and provides various advanced capabilities.
It combines powerful under-the-hood AI with a polished, fluid user interface that makes managing your recordings feel effortless. Whether you are sorting through voice notes or analyzing long meetings, Jotist provides a beautiful environment to get work done:

- **Smart Speaker Detection**: It doesn’t just output a block of text—Jotist automatically detects different speakers (Diarization) and labels exactly who said what.
- **Chat with your Audio**: Connect seamlessly with Ollama or OpenAI. You can generate summaries, ask questions, or have a full conversation with your transcripts right inside the app.
- **Built for your Workflow**: With extensive APIs and a clever "File Watcher" that automatically processes new files in a folder, Jotist fits right into your existing automations (like n8n).
- **Capture & Organize**: Use the built-in audio recorder to capture thoughts on the fly, and use the integrated note-taking features to annotate your transcripts as you listen.
- **Native Experience everywhere**: Jotist supports PWA (Progressive Web App) installation, giving you a native app experience on your desktop or mobile device.
- **A Polished UI**: Dark mode, synchronized playback, and responsive controls help keep long recordings manageable.
